# Accepting online payments on invoices
Connect Yoco or PayFast at Settings > Payments to add a "Pay now" button to your invoices. Card payments settle into your account according to your provider's payout schedule, not Kettle's.
Invoices are marked Paid automatically when the provider confirms the payment, usually within minutes. If an invoice stays Unpaid after the client paid, check the payment appears in your Yoco or PayFast dashboard, then mark it paid manually from the invoice menu.
Kettle does not hold your money and cannot reverse client payments; refunds to your clients are done in your payment provider's dashboard.
