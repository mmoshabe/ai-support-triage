# Plans and pricing
Kettle has three plans, billed monthly in advance on the date you first subscribed:
- Starter: R149/month, 1 user, up to 20 invoices per month.
- Growth: R349/month, up to 5 users, unlimited invoices, bank sync.
- Pro: R699/month, unlimited users, unlimited invoices, bank sync, recurring invoices and API access.

Annual billing gives two months free. Prices include VAT. To switch between monthly and annual billing, go to Settings > Billing > Change plan and choose the billing period; the switch takes effect at your next billing date.
To change plan go to Settings > Billing > Change plan. Upgrades take effect immediately and you are charged a pro-rated amount for the rest of the current month. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period.
