# Refund policy
Monthly subscriptions: you can cancel at any time and you will not be charged again, but we do not refund partial months.
Annual subscriptions: if you cancel within 14 days of the annual charge you get a full refund. After 14 days annual plans are not refundable.
Duplicate charges: if you were charged twice for the same period, we refund the duplicate in full. Refunds take 5-10 business days to appear on your statement.
Refund requests are reviewed by the billing team; support cannot issue refunds directly. Reply to your ticket with the date and amount of the charge and the billing team will confirm by email.
