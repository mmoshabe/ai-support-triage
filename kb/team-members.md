# Adding and removing team members
Account owners and admins can invite people at Settings > Team > Invite. Invites expire after 7 days; resend from the same screen.
Roles: Owner (one per account, controls billing), Admin (everything except billing), Member (create and send invoices), Viewer (read-only).
Removing a team member takes effect immediately. The number of users you can add depends on your plan (see Plans and pricing).
To transfer account ownership, the current owner goes to Settings > Team, opens the new owner's profile and clicks "Make owner".
