# Recurring invoices
Recurring invoices are available on the Pro plan. Open any invoice and click More > Make recurring, then choose the frequency (weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly) and the start date.
Each new invoice is created at 06:00 SAST on its due date and emailed to the client automatically if "Auto-send" is on.
To stop a schedule, go to Invoices > Recurring and click Pause or Delete. Pausing keeps the schedule so you can resume later.
