# What happens when your card payment fails
If Kettle cannot charge your card we retry automatically after 3 days and again after 7 days. You get an email each time.
Your account stays fully active during the 14 days after the first failed attempt. After 14 days the account becomes read-only: you can still view and download invoices but cannot create new ones.
To fix it, go to Settings > Billing > Payment method, add a new card, then click "Retry payment". Access is restored as soon as the payment succeeds.
Common causes: expired card, insufficient funds, or your bank blocking an online transaction. Kettle charges appear on statements as "KETTLE APP".
