# Cancelling your subscription
Account owners can cancel at Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. Only the account owner can cancel.
After cancelling, your account stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, then becomes read-only for 90 days so you can export your data. After 90 days the data is deleted.
To export everything, go to Settings > Data > Export, which emails you a ZIP of all invoices (PDF) and a CSV of clients and payments.
