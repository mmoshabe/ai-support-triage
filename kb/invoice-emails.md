# Invoice emails not reaching clients
Check the invoice's Activity tab: "Delivered" means the client's mail server accepted it, "Bounced" means the address is wrong or the mailbox is full.
If delivered but not seen, ask your client to check spam and to add invoices@kettle.app to their contacts.
To resend, open the invoice and click Send > Resend. You can also click "Copy link" to share the invoice link directly via WhatsApp or another channel.
