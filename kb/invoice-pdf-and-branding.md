# Invoice PDFs, logos and branding
Upload your logo at Settings > Branding (PNG or JPG, max 2 MB). It appears on all invoices created after upload; to update an existing invoice, open it and click Regenerate PDF.
You can choose a brand colour, footer text and payment terms on the same screen.
If a PDF shows a blank page or missing logo, clear your browser cache and click Regenerate PDF. If it still fails, note the invoice number and reply to support.
